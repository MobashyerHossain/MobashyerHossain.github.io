Build or buy

EchoNex, middleware for payment speakers

Click to untangle

The problem
When a customer paid by QR code, the merchant and the customer both had to check a phone to know the money had arrived.
The two options
Management had decided to hand the platform to a third party. I believed our own team could build it and keep the product in-house.
The call
I made the case to management, won it, and led the build: a platform that connects speaker devices to the payment gateway and manages which merchant each device belongs to, with reporting in one place.
The result
Payments are announced out loud at the counter. The platform has earned the company millions of taka.

Dependency

POS host simulator, built in Python

Click to untangle

The problem
To test a transaction, the team either read device logs line by line or connected to a bank’s host, which was only possible when the bank was available.
The call
Build a simulator that behaves like the bank’s host, so any developer can run a full transaction at their desk.
The result
Day-to-day testing no longer depends on a bank. It has saved the team days of development time.

Speed

webOS payment app for the Topwise Q1

Click to untangle

The problem
A bank needed a payment application on a terminal platform that was new to us. A build like that normally takes three to four months.
The call
I wrote what I know about payment applications into a reusable skill for Claude, then built the app with AI as a working partner instead of a search engine.
The result
The app was developed in two weeks.

Cost

ECR launcher, connecting cash registers to payment apps

Click to untangle

The problem
Linking a merchant’s cash register to the payment app on a terminal relied on a manufacturer’s launcher that carries a licence cost.
The call
Build our own launcher that speaks the same protocols.
The result
Our customers get a premium service at no extra cost.
