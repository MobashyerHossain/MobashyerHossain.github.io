Case A · Build or buy · EchoNex, middleware for payment speakers

The problem
When a customer paid by QR code, the merchant and the customer both had to check a phone to know the money had arrived.
The two options
Management had decided to hand the platform to a third party. I believed our own team could build it and keep the product in-house.
The call
I made the case to management, won it, and led the build: a platform that takes payment notifications from the payment gateway and sends them to speaker devices through the device manager, keeps track of which merchant each device belongs to, and reports in one place.
The result
Payments are announced out loud at the counter, on about 10,000 payment speakers. The platform has earned the company millions of taka.

Our testing ran on the bank’s schedule, not ours.

Case B · Dependency · POS host simulator, built in Python

The problem
To test a transaction, the team either read device logs line by line or connected to a bank’s host, which was only possible when the bank was available.
The call
Build a simulator that behaves like the bank’s host, so any developer can run a full transaction at their desk.
The result
Day-to-day testing no longer depends on a bank. It has saved the team days of development time.

A three to four month build, delivered in two weeks.

Case C · Speed · webOS payment app for the Topwise Q1

The problem
A bank needed a payment application on a terminal platform that was new to us. A build like that normally takes three to four months.
The call
I wrote what I know about payment applications into a reusable skill for Claude, then built the app with AI as a working partner instead of a search engine.
The result
The app was developed in two weeks.

Customers were going to pay a licence fee for something we could give them.

Case D · Cost · E-Connect, the ECR launcher connecting cash registers to payment apps

The problem
Linking a merchant’s cash register to the payment app on a terminal relied on a manufacturer’s launcher that carries a licence cost.
The call
Build our own launcher that speaks the same protocols, starting with prototypes over USB, serial and Bluetooth and finishing as E-Connect.
The result
Our customers get a premium service at no extra cost.

A speaker that announces payments, and soon takes them.

Case E · New product · Payment speaker applications for the CS85

The problem
A payment speaker tells a merchant that money has arrived. Merchants also needed the same small device to accept the payment.
The call
Building the applications for the CS85 speaker, including the ability to pay by phone. This work is in development now.
The result
In development: one device on the counter that will take the payment and confirm it out loud.

The team could move faster. Management needed convincing.

Case F · People · Bringing AI tools into the department

The problem
AI tools were not part of how the company built software.
The call
I made the argument to management for an AI-friendly way of working and for Claude Pro for every developer on my team.
The result
The team got the tools, and its productivity rose sharply.

The database was at 100% CPU. The fix wasn’t a bigger server.

Case G · Performance · EchoNex database, MySQL

The problem
As the payment speaker network grew, EchoNex’s MySQL database ran at about 100% CPU on a 12-core server.
The two options
Give the database more resources, or find out why the queries were so expensive.
The call
Optimised the queries and indexes instead of adding hardware.
The result
Database CPU fell from about 100% to 10–15%, on the same server.

North South University, 2023.

Thesis: Transforming the Dhaka Stock Exchange: Leveraging Reinforcement Learning for Automated Trading and Predictive Insights. Tested reinforcement learning trading agents on Dhaka Stock Exchange price history; Deep Q-Learning and Double Q-Learning performed best.

Abstract

This research explores the application of Reinforcement Learning (RL) techniques, utilizing agents such as Simple Moving Average, Signal Rolling Agent, Policy Gradient, Deep Q-Learning, and Double Q-Learning, for autonomous trading and predictive analysis in the context of the Dhaka Stock Exchange (DSE). Our primary focus is on the adaptability and profitability advantages that RL-based agents offer over static methods. By harnessing historical closing prices from the DSE, we demonstrate the potential of RL to enhance trading strategies and improve stock price forecasts, with specific emphasis on the superior performance of the Deep Q-Learning and Double Q-Learning agent. This study represents a novel experiment in DSE stock data, showcasing RL's effectiveness in adapting to the dynamic and stochastic nature of financial markets. Furthermore, the methodologies introduced in this paper not only augment trading strategies but also provide valuable insights and guidance for stock market analysis. This research underscores the growing importance of AI-driven decision-making in finance and highlights the significant role of RL in shaping trading and prediction strategies for autonomous trading agents in stock exchange settings.

Thesis: Augmented Reality Based Mobile Virtual Green Room. A mobile app that lets shoppers try clothes on in augmented reality, with a market analysis of its benefits and drawbacks for buyers and sellers.

Abstract

With the increase of trade in the fashion industry, comes the increased need for people to try out their wears before or during purchase. In some cases, trying out several dresses before purchasing only one or sometimes even none at all. This raises the problem of limited showroom space which causes number of trial rooms in each showroom to be limited. And for e-commerce web App the problem of unsatisfied customers for lack of trial before purchase is more evident. This project tackles this problem by using the current available Augmented Reality technology to create a mobile virtual trial room. This trial room comes in the form of mobile App and can be accessed from anywhere with a Smartphone, HoloLens or Google glass. This project also provides a detailed market analysis about the pros and cons of using this App and also predicts the future benefits and loses for both the consumer and the seller.
